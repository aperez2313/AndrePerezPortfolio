## Decisions so far

### Section 13 of the brand guide (all approved by Andre)
1. Dark mode: none, no toggle. Dark sections in Night supply the 20% Night share.
2. Status colors: no contact form at launch (email and LinkedIn links only). If a form comes later: errors are words plus Night on Lilac, success is Night on Citron.
3. Metric display: stat block. Number in Sora 600, with baseline, timeframe, and what changed in Manrope beneath. If no defensible number, the block holds a before/after sentence. Never a chart.
4. Motif: keep the Lilac dot (every kit file uses it). "Once per piece" means once per page. Add a Night version (Citron bars, Lilac dot).
5. Motion: hover underline, visible focus, one short motif build-in on Home. No scroll fade-ins. Honor prefers-reduced-motion.
6. White surface: dropped. Cards use a 1px Lilac or Night border on Bone.
7. Label tracking: 0.08em (guide wins over styles.css).
8. Descriptor: "Business operations / Digital commerce / Practical technology" (the kit line). Provisional, may change with the next resume. Job titles always match the resume exactly ("eCom Manager", "Ecommerce Manager").

### Resume workflow (approved)
- Source of truth: the single `.docx` in `andre-perez-site/content/resume/`. The build converts it into the Resume page and the downloadable PDF. Andre never edits site code for a resume update.
- The current DOCX was generated by ChatGPT. It has no Word heading styles and no real list items: section names are bold all-caps lines, job titles are bold lines, employer lines are "Employer | City | Start - End", bullets are typed "•" characters. The converter must accept this format and the standard Word format (Heading 1/2/3 plus List Bullet). Tables, columns, and text boxes are unsupported.
